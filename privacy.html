Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the website at jamezboi.github.io ("the Site") and the applications it hosts, including InstaTrack Pro Web and the AetherScan web console (together, "the Services"), handle information. By using the Services, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

InstaTrack Pro Web

  • Account information: an email address and password when you create an account. Passwords are hashed and managed by our authentication provider (Supabase); we never see or store your plaintext password.
  • Instagram username: the Instagram username you connect to your account.
  • Instagram session data: when you connect your Instagram account, an encrypted session token is created so scans can run. It is stored encrypted and is never displayed or logged.
  • Scan results: follower counts, follower/unfollower/new-follower username lists, and scan status history associated with your account.

AetherScan Web Console

  • Demo mode: everything runs locally in your browser against a simulated network. No scan data is collected or transmitted.
  • Live mode: the console proxies scan requests only to the AetherScan engine running on your own computer. Scan data stays between your browser and your machine.
  • Google Sign-In (optional): if you sign in with Google, your name, email address, and profile picture are read from the Google ID token and verified locally in your browser. This information is not stored on our servers.

All visitors

The Site is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect standard server logs (such as IP address and request details) when you visit. See GitHub's Privacy Statement for details.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide the core functionality of the Services (accounts, scans, scan history, dashboards).
  • To authenticate you and secure your account.
  • To troubleshoot issues you report.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information for advertising. The Services contain no ads and no analytics/tracking scripts.

3. Data Storage & Security

  • InstaTrack account and scan data are stored in Supabase (PostgreSQL) protected by row-level security, so each user can only access their own data.
  • Instagram session data is stored encrypted and never displayed or logged. Your Instagram password is transmitted over HTTPS only to establish a session and is not stored.
  • AetherScan demo and license verification run entirely client-side in your browser.

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

4. Third-Party Services

  • GitHub Pages — static hosting of the Site.
  • Supabase — authentication and database for InstaTrack.
  • Google — optional sign-in for AetherScan (governed by Google's Privacy Policy).
  • Instagram / Meta — accessed only when you choose to connect your own Instagram account for scanning.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Your data is retained while your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time using the contact information below, and we will delete it promptly. Deleting your account removes your email, Instagram username, session data, and scan history.

6. Children's Privacy

The Services are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the Services after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to request data deletion, contact us via GitHub: github.com/Jamezboi.

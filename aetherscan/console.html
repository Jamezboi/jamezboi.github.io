Scanning

          

            
Default port profile
Applied when scanning unless overridden.

            
          

          

            
Ping timeout
Higher values find sleepy devices but slow sweeps.

            
          

          

            
Online vendor lookup
Query macvendors.com for OUIs missing from the offline database. Sends MAC prefixes to a third party.

            
          

        


        

          
Discovery depth

          

            
TCP sweep for silent devices
After the ping sweep, probe common ports on hosts that never answered — catches firewalled laptops, headless IoT and printers. Ultimate.

            
          

          

            
Demo devices
Seeded sample devices can overlap with a real network. Purge them from the inventory entirely.

            
          

        


        

          
Security audit

          

            
Factory-credential audit
Opt-in. Tries 5 classic default logins (read-only) against admin pages on your own devices. Never enables anything, never changes state.

About

          
AetherScan

          
Built by James Laliberte · site & console ·
             reach out at Jamezboyyy@gmail.com for help or inquiries.

          
AetherScan performs only non-invasive checks against devices it
             discovers. It never attempts exploits beyond the opt-in factory-credential audit,
             and it is designed exclusively for networks you own or are authorized to assess.
             Always follow local law and your ISP's terms.

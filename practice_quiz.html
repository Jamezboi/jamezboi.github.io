General Chemistry Formula & Reference Sheet

Uncertainty & Statistics

Sample standard deviation: $s = \sqrt{\frac{\sum (x_i - \bar{x})^2}{N - 1}}$

Percent Error: $\text{\% Error} = \frac{|\text{Experimental} - \text{Theoretical}|}{\text{Theoretical}} \times 100\%$

Atomic Physics & Rydberg

Rydberg Equation: $\frac{1}{\lambda} = R_H \left( \frac{1}{n_1^2} - \frac{1}{n_2^2} \right)$ where $R_H = 1.097 \times 10^7 \text{ m}^{-1}$

Bohr Energy: $\Delta E = -2.18 \times 10^{-18} \text{ J} \left( \frac{Z^2}{n_f^2} - \frac{Z^2}{n_i^2} \right)$

de Broglie wavelength: $\lambda = \frac{h}{p} = \frac{h}{m v}$ where $h = 6.626 \times 10^{-34} \text{ J s}$

Quantum Number Constraints

  • Principal Quantum Number: $n = 1, 2, 3, \dots, \infty$
  • Angular Momentum Quantum Number: $l = 0, 1, 2, \dots, n-1$ ($s=0, p=1, d=2, f=3$)
  • Magnetic Quantum Number: $m_l = -l, \dots, 0, \dots, +l$
  • Spin Quantum Number: $m_s = +\frac{1}{2}, -\frac{1}{2}$

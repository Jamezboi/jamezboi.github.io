🔒 PRIVACY BY DESIGN

Your Data Never Leaves Your Computer

Sentinel operates completely local-first. There are no accounts, no cloud servers, and no remote telemetry.

  • 🛡️ Desk-Only Camera Crop: Discards face & upper body; only inspects desk surface for phones.
  • 💾 Local SQLite Database: All score curves, sessions, and events stored in sentinel.db.
  • 📊 Post-Session HTML Debriefs: Interactive cyber-tactical reports generated directly to your disk.
  • 🛡️ Process Whitelist Safeguards: Never affects system apps like explorer.exe or dwm.exe.

Visible interventions

PRIVACY BY DESIGN

Your focus data stays on your computer.

Sentinel has no account, no telemetry, and no remote dashboard. It stores local scores, training labels, and—only after a confirmed violation—one locally queued evidence image.

Desk mode safeguards
  • Camera upper region is discarded before detection.
  • No live camera preview in the console.
  • No face, gaze, posture, or drowsiness processing.
  • Evidence upload is off until you configure a destination.
TRAIN IT ONCE

Your rules, remembered.

Use “Mark as work” or “Mark as distraction” for the active app. Optional local Chrome debug mode allows domain-specific allow and block lists.

LOCAL ANALYTICS

See the pattern. Change the pattern.

The localhost dashboard shows score trends, focus states, distraction sources, evidence queue status, and training data—without sending it anywhere.
